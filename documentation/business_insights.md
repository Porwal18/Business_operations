# Business Insights Report

## Executive Summary

Analysis of 50,000 operational tickets identified a 16.14% overall SLA breach rate.

The analysis indicates that SLA performance varies significantly by department, process, priority, and workload level.

The highest operational risks are concentrated in Compliance, Order Management, Procurement, critical-priority tickets, and high-workload periods.

---

## Key Findings

### 1. Overall SLA Performance

- Total tickets analyzed: 50,000
- SLA met: 41,930
- SLA breached: 8,070
- Overall SLA breach rate: 16.14%

Approximately one in six operational tickets breaches the defined SLA.

---

### 2. Department Performance

Compliance has the highest department-level SLA breach rate at 32.36%, followed by:

- Order Management: 24.44%
- Procurement: 24.34%
- Finance Operations: 12.94%
- IT Support: 10.69%

Compliance therefore represents the highest-priority department for further process investigation.

---

### 3. Process Performance

The highest-risk processes are:

| Process | SLA Breach Rate | Avg Resolution |
|---|---:|---:|
| Order Exception Management | 41.90% | 47.40 hrs |
| Compliance Review | 37.11% | 45.18 hrs |
| Purchase Order Processing | 29.35% | 43.26 hrs |
| Documentation Review | 27.63% | 43.12 hrs |
| Payment Processing | 21.80% | 41.61 hrs |

Order Exception Management represents the highest process-level SLA risk.

---

### 4. Priority Analysis

Critical tickets have a 45.25% SLA breach rate compared with:

- High: 16.50%
- Medium: 11.76%
- Low: 17.81%

Critical requests therefore require further investigation into staffing, assignment delays, SLA targets, and escalation processes.

---

### 5. Workload Analysis

High workload is strongly associated with poorer SLA performance.

| Workload Level | SLA Breach Rate | Avg Resolution |
|---|---:|---:|
| High | 42.97% | 47.69 hrs |
| Medium | 6.71% | 37.55 hrs |
| Low | 1.10% | 31.95 hrs |

The difference suggests that workload management should be investigated as a potential contributor to SLA breaches.

This finding represents an association and should not be interpreted as proof of causation without further analysis.

---

## Recommended Business Actions

### Priority 1 — Investigate Order Exception Management

Review:

- Process steps
- Approval requirements
- Manual activities
- Assignment delays
- Workload distribution
- Escalation points

### Priority 2 — Review Compliance Processes

Perform a detailed process and GAP analysis of:

- Compliance Review
- Documentation Review

### Priority 3 — Review High-Workload Capacity

Investigate whether additional capacity, workload balancing, automation, or queue-management mechanisms could improve SLA performance during high-workload periods.

### Priority 4 — Critical Ticket Management

Review the handling of critical tickets from:

Ticket Creation → Assignment → Processing → Escalation → Resolution

---

## Analytical Limitations

The dataset is synthetically generated for portfolio and analytical demonstration purposes.

The findings demonstrate the analytical methodology and should not be interpreted as actual business performance.

Correlation or association between variables does not independently establish causation.
